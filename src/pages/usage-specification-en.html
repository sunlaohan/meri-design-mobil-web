Layout

Control Baseline Settings

iPhone selects 375*812pt @1x as the design baseline, adapting upwards to @2x, @3x.

Android selects 360*720dp QHD as the design baseline, adapting upwards to xhdpi, xxhdpi, xxxhdpi.

Layout Spacing

Spacing design principles follow a unified 4pt grid system, using multiples of 4 for spacing, such as 4, 8, 16, 24, 32. This is suitable for most mobile screen sizes. Establish a hierarchy of spacing levels to accommodate different scenarios.

In the overall spacing for mobile, all designs are based on multiples of 4, making the overall interface cleaner and more orderly.

Spacing Settings

Our spacing only requires an outer margin of 16px; designers in the content area can define a grid system that suits the product based on business characteristics.
